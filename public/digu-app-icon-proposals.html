Android Adaptive Layer Pipeline (108dp System)

How your chosen icon is exported into the production Android project without clipping or resolution loss.

1. Vector Foreground (72dp safe circle)

The primary artwork (the Dhivehi ޑ, spade crest, cards) sits strictly inside the 72dp center safe zone so no OEM squircle or circular mask clips any contour.

2. Deep Lacquer Background (108dp canvas)

The 108dp background fills all edges with royal lacquer ground (#0d0605) and radial warmth, supporting smooth parallax effects during Android launcher animations.

3. Multi-Density Mipmap Export (hdpi to xxxhdpi)

Exports crisp PNGs for mdpi (48px), hdpi (72px), xhdpi (96px), xxhdpi (144px), xxxhdpi (192px), plus 512px Play Store master, replacing default Capacitor placeholders.
