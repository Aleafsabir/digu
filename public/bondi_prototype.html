BONDI OFFICIAL RULES

Core Objective: Shed all your cards. The last player left holding cards is The Bondi (the loser).

Player Count: 2 to 6 players, free-for-all cutthroat (no partnerships). Anti-clockwise play.

Opening Trick: Player holding the Ace of Spades (A♠) leads Trick 1 (with any card).

Must Follow Suit: You must follow the led suit if you hold at least one card of that suit.

Clean Round: If all active players follow suit cleanly, the entire pile is burned, and the highest card player leads next.

Giving Bondi: If you hold no cards in the led suit, you must give Bondi!

  • Standard Bondi: Play 1 off-suit card. The player with the highest card of the led suit takes the entire pile into their hand!
  • Suit Bondi (Mass Dump): Select an off-suit and dump every card of that suit. The highest card player absorbs the entire massive pile!

The player who receives the Bondi (the player who held the highest card and absorbed the pile) leads the next trick.
