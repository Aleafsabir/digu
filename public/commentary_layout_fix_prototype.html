❌ The Current Bug (Screenshot)

  • Commentary was placed at fixed top-14 (56px) while opponents row sits at top-1 inside <main> (~52px).
  • The commentary pill lands on the exact same vertical and horizontal space as the middle opponent badges.
  • Player names and card counts are occluded, destroying situational awareness.

✨ Proposed Layout A: Docked Arena Pill

  • Commentary pill docks to the top rim of the Center Trick Arena (-top-3.5).
  • Visually unifies round state (e.g. "Naeem leads") directly with the card pile below it.
  • Guarantees zero overlap with opponent badges above, preserving clear sightlines regardless of phone screen ratio.
