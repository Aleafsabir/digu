🎯 DPI-Agnostic Vector Precision
  • Zero Raster Pixelation: All card pips (♠, ♥, ♦, ♣), crowns, badges, and icons are pure mathematical SVG vector paths that render infinitely sharp at @1x, @2x, @3x, and @4x screen densities.
  • Crisp Border Radii & Lines: Borders use explicit physical dp equivalents (1px and 1.5px) rather than sub-pixel hairlines (0.5px) that blur on 1.5x/2.5x Android displays.
👆 Ergonomic Touch-Target Floor Compliance
  • Apple HIG Compliance: Every interactive element (cards, buttons, dump selectors) meets or exceeds Apple's mandated 44 × 44 pt touch target boundary.
  • Google Material Compliance: Meets Android's 48 × 48 dp thumb-reach zone, ensuring easy actuation even on dense 480–640 dpi screens without mis-taps.

⚠️ Screen Size Bottlenecks Fixed
  • Fixed Arena Height: Previous h-52 (208px) forced total screen height over 380px, causing the bottom card hand to get squeezed off-screen on phones like the iPhone 15/16 landscape or Galaxy S24.
  • Notch Clipping: West and East player badges lacked safe area margins, causing Dynamic Island or camera punch holes to obscure player names.
✅ Responsive Layout Formulas
  • Fluid Center Trick Arena: Uses clamp(135px, 36dvh, 195px) height so the arena scales smoothly with any viewport height.
  • Dynamic 13-Card Spacing: Uses negative margin clamp (-space-x-5 to -space-x-7.5) and adaptive card width (clamp(34px, 5.5vw, 56px)) so 13 cards fit comfortably across all landscape aspect ratios.
  • Safe Area Insets: Applied horizontal safe-area padding to the main arena container to shield West/East badges from notches.
