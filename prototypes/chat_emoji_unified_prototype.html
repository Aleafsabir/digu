Interactive Reaction Testing Deck Tap any button to test popup visibility, duration & sender attribution

❌ Previous Architecture

Two buttons on both sides: Quick Chat on the Left rail and Emojis on the Right rail, even though clicking either opened a modal that contained both tabs.

Tiny 2.6s popup: Fleeting 2600ms duration caused players to miss reactions while focusing on their hand.

No sender name: Bubbles floated anonymously without indicating whether You or an AI opponent sent the phrase.

✓ Proposed Architecture

Consolidated to one ergonomic side: A single luxury pill button (Left or Right thumb rail) containing both Chat & Emoji, reducing visual clutter by 50%.

High-visibility 4.5s popup: Deep lacquer border, 32px animated emojis, 14px bold typography, and visual countdown timer.

Explicit sender badge: High-contrast pill badges (YOU, HASSAN, etc.) and directional speech tails unmistakably indicate who is speaking.
